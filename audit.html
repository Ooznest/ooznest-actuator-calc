Audit rule

Every published source needs its product URL or product ID, date checked, rating direction and whether it is already safety-factored. Values labelled as modelling assumptions need a test plan or a conservative justification.

Open model issue

The current gantry/wheel data does not yet distinguish plate-level Cy from individual wheel radial capacity. The existing graph and wheel card must not treat a plate rating as a rating per wheel.

These pages are documentation/audit outputs only; they do not change calculator inputs.
