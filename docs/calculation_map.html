1. Data and user inputs

Motor data

motors.json

Attributes: torque, current, resistance, inductance, steps. These create the torque-versus-speed estimate.

Actuator data

transmission.json

Attributes: type, lead, efficiency, friction. These convert motor torque into linear force.

Gantry plate data

gantryplates.json

Attributes: mass, actual.radialStaticN, actual.axialStaticNByRailMm, actual.myStaticNm, actual.mzStaticNm.

Motion inputsLength, orientation, requested speed and acceleration.
Load inputsPayload, payload-centre offset and plate orientation.
Drive inputsMotor, driver current, microstepping and factor of safety.

2. Calculate available drive force at the requested speed

Motor speed

RPM = speed ÷ actuator.lead

The selected linear speed is converted to motor RPM.

User: speed
Data: transmission.json → lead

Electrical speed limit

headroom = max(0.4, (24 − I×R) ÷ 22.5)no-load RPM = 687.5 × √(4÷L) × √headroom × max(0.5, I÷1.25)^0.15

24 V supply, winding resistance and inductance estimate current build-up.

User: driver current
Data: motors.json → resistance, inductance

Running motor torque

speed fraction = max(0, 1 − (RPM ÷ no-load RPM)^exponent)torque = holding torque × 0.0980665 × current ratio × fraction × derate

Reference 345 oz-in screw: exponent 2.2, derate 1.0. Other combinations: 1.65 and 0.75.

Data: motors.json → torque, current
Model constants: index.js

Safe linear force

screw: torque × efficiency × 2π ÷ lead(m)belt: torque × efficiency ÷ (lead(m) ÷ 2π)design force = raw force ÷ FOS

Motor/drive force available at the requested speed.

Data: transmission.json → type, efficiency, lead
User: FOS

3. Calculate the force the requested move needs

Total moving mass

moving mass = payload + selected plate mass

The selected plate mass is added automatically. Payload is all user-added moving mass.

User: payload
Data: gantryplates.json → mass

Gravity and friction

gravity = mass × 9.80665 × sin(θ)friction = mass × 9.80665 × friction coefficient × cos(θ)

θ is 90° for a vertical lift and 0° for horizontal motion.

User: actuator orientation
Data: transmission.json → friction

Acceleration demand

inertia = mass × acceleration ÷ 1000

Acceleration is entered in mm/s², then converted to m/s².

User: acceleration

Required force

required force = gravity + friction + inertiaforce margin = design force − required force

This is compared with available drive force at the requested speed.

Calculated in index.js → requirement()

4. Independent mechanical caps

Gantry plate load

Radial (Vertical): actual.radialStaticN.
Axial (Horizontal): actual.axialStaticNByRailMm[railWidth].

usable plate load = selected actual rating ÷ FOSplate demand = moving mass × (9.80665 + acceleration ÷ 1000)

PASS when demand is no more than usable plate load.

Payload moment

payload moment = payload × (9.80665 + acceleration ÷ 1000) × offset(m)usable moment = min(actual My, actual Mz) ÷ FOS

PASS when payload moment is no more than usable moment. The more conservative of My/Mz is used.

5. At the requested speed: pass/fail decision

  • Motor torque: design drive force ≥ required force.
  • Speed capability: requested speed ≤ calculated maximum speed.
  • Gantry plate: plate demand ≤ usable plate load.
  • Moment load: payload moment ≤ usable moment.

Any failure = Not recommended. A low force margin gives a warning even when all checks pass.
